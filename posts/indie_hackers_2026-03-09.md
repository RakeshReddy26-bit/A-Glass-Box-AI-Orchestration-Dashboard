This week I dove deep into why AI career platforms are exploding right now, and honestly, the timing couldn't be better for what we're building at SkillVector.

Anthropic just launched Claude Career Coach and OpenAI dropped GPT-Jobs - both AI-native career platforms trained specifically on technical roles. This validates what we've been seeing: traditional career advice is too slow for how fast tech roles are evolving.

The challenge I'm wrestling with? Our Neo4j knowledge graph is getting massive. We're tracking relationships between skills, companies, job requirements, and career paths in real-time. The data is incredibly rich, but query performance is becoming a bottleneck.

Here's what I learned: ML engineers don't just want to know what skills to learn - they want to understand the exact technical interview patterns, the GitHub contribution expectations, and the specific deployment scenarios they'll face. Generic advice doesn't cut it anymore.

We're using Claude Sonnet to analyze job postings and extract these nuanced requirements, then storing the relationships in our graph database. It's technically complex but the insights are incredible.
