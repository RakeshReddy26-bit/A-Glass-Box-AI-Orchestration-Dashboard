This week I learned something unsettling while building SkillVector: we're solving a problem that's accelerating faster than I anticipated.

Anthropicjust launched 'Claude Career Coach' - an AI that analyzes GitHub repos and creates personalized learning paths for ML engineers. It's a direct competitor to what we're building, but honestly, I'm excited rather than worried.

Here's why: they're validating the exact problem we identified. AI professionals need real-time career intelligence, not static job boards or generic courses. The fact that a company like Anthropic is investing in this space confirms we're on the right track.

But there's a challenge I didn't expect. Our users want hyper-personalized insights, but they're skeptical of AI analyzing their career data. It's ironic - ML engineers who build AI systems are hesitant to use AI for their own career planning.

We're solving this by being transparent about our methodology and giving users control over their data. The key insight: career intelligence needs to feel collaborative, not automated.

The competition validates the market, but execution and trust will differentiate us. Sometimes the best validation comes from seeing others tackle the same problem you've been obsessing over.
